About the Press

Serious inquiry
into unsettled questions

Vigilia Press is an independent nonfiction imprint dedicated to subjects that demand rigorous investigation — and that the broader publishing industry too often declines to examine with the seriousness they deserve.

We publish authors who have done the work: primary sources, named witnesses, documentary evidence, and the difficult questions that follow. Our titles are written for readers who are neither believers nor dismissers, but who understand that some questions matter precisely because they remain open.

The press takes its name from the Latin for watchfulness — the long vigil, the careful witness, the record of what was seen.

I

Rigor

Every claim supported. Every source named where possible. The standard of serious journalism applied without apology to subjects others find uncomfortable.

II

Measure

The facts do the work. Vigilia Press titles neither sensationalize nor dismiss. The tone is that of a careful historian confronting primary evidence.

III

Independence

No institutional pressure. No editorial agenda beyond the truth of the record. The press exists to publish what deserves to be published.

Over-Classified

Ridicule and Managed Ignorance

David R. Brown

A rigorously sourced investigation into the classification architecture surrounding unidentified anomalous phenomena, sustained for decades through ridicule and managed ignorance. Drawing on government documents, congressional testimony, and firsthand accounts from whistleblowers, Over-classified examines what the evidence actually shows, what remains deliberately concealed, and why the fight for disclosure matters now more than ever.

About the Author

David R. Brown spent three decades in enterprise risk management, retiring in 2020 as a senior executive responsible for casualty claims programs across large organizations. His career was built on mapping where accountability breaks down and where institutional safeguards fail — the same discipline that shapes this book. That gap between what the government knows and what it admits is the story Over-classified was written to tell. He lives in South Carolina.

The Imprint

An independent press
for the careful witness

Vigilia Press was founded on Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, to publish serious nonfiction on subjects that mainstream publishing has declined to examine rigorously — not because the evidence is absent, but because the subjects themselves carry institutional resistance.

The name comes from the Latin for watchfulness. The vigil — the long night's keeping of attention — is both the method and the posture of every title we publish.
